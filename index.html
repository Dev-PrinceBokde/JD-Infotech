05CCTV AMC contract

A camera that isn't recording is just decoration.

Hard disks fill up, adapters burn out, lenses get dusty and passwords get forgotten. Nobody notices until something happens. An Annual Maintenance Contract puts regular checks on the calendar, and that applies whether we installed your system or someone else did.

Ask about AMC Call us
AMC visit checklist—
  • Camera health & viewing angles
  • Lens & housing cleaning
  • DVR / NVR recording verified
  • Hard disk status & backup days
  • Power supply, adapters & connectors
  • Mobile app & remote view tested
  • Priority attention on breakdowns

08FAQs

Common questions.

Still unsure? A quick call usually sorts it out.

Ask on WhatsApp
Will you visit the site before quoting?

For most jobs, yes. Seeing the premises lets us suggest the right number of cameras and where to place them, so the quote matches what actually gets installed.

Can I watch the cameras on my mobile?

Yes. If the site has an internet connection, we set up the DVR/NVR for live and recorded viewing on your phone, and we configure the app with you before handover.

Dome, bullet or IP camera: which is right for me?

Domes suit indoor spaces. Bullets suit outdoor walls and longer distances. IP cameras give higher clarity and are easier to expand later. We'll recommend a mix based on your site, not a fixed package.

Do you take AMC for systems installed by someone else?

Yes. We inspect the existing setup first, fix what's needed, and then put it on a regular maintenance schedule.

Will I get a GST bill?

Yes. JD Infotech is GST-registered (24AASFJ4222D1ZL), so societies, offices and businesses get a proper tax invoice.

What should I send for a quick quote?

Send the property type, your area in Ahmedabad and roughly how many spots you want covered. A few photos on WhatsApp help a lot.
